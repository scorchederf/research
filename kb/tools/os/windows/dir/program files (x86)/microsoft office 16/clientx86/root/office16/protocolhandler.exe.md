## simple description

> ### 2026-11-21 Claude said
> ProtocolHandler.exe is a Microsoft Office component that handles Office URI schemes such as ms-word:, ms-excel: and ms-powerpoint:. When a browser or app opens such a link, ProtocolHandler.exe parses it and starts the right Office application with the document URL. It is installed with Click-to-Run Office and runs briefly when those links are clicked.
>
> **SOC NOTE**: Office URI links can be used in phishing to open remote documents directly in Office (T1204), and ProtocolHandler.exe is a LOLBin that can be pointed at an attacker-controlled URL to download/open content. Investigate ProtocolHandler.exe launched with unusual URLs, followed by Office child processes.
>
